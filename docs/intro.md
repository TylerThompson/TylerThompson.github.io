# About Tyler Thompson

I'm a senior software engineer and founder based in Evansville, Wisconsin.
For fifteen years I've built production systems: enterprise observability
platforms at Northwestern Mutual, then my own products through Thompson
Software.

## What I do

- Design, build, and ship web and mobile software end to end
- Run Thompson Software, an independent studio for custom apps, SaaS products,
  and browser extensions
- Ship my own products, including LiveFromWisco, Luna, the Julian Suite, and
  two browser extensions

## Selected work

- **LiveFromWisco** — community platform used by 28,000+ Wisconsinites
  across web, iOS, and Android.
- **Julian Suite** — six privacy-first services, each standalone:
  Julian Notes (encrypted note-taking), Julian Meetings (peer-to-peer video),
  Julian Chat (encrypted messaging), Julian Storage (cloud storage),
  Julian Auth (two-factor auth), and Julian Vault (passwords). In
  development.
- **Luna** — private cycle tracker, live on iOS and Android.
- **IconForge** and **Daycare Cost Calculator** — browser extensions live in
  the Chrome Web Store.
- **SocialFlare** — founded and operated a social networking platform for
  more than six years.
- **google-play-console-mcp** and **Veya** — open-source projects.

## Background

Before founding Thompson Software, I led enterprise monitoring and
observability work at Northwestern Mutual across HP BSM, New Relic,
Dynatrace, Splunk, and Elastic. Earlier still, I spent years in
product engineering and QA — software engineer at FatWallet, Ebates, and
Rakuten (staying through Ebates' acquisition of FatWallet and Rakuten's
acquisition of Ebates), and QA engineer at Grubhub. That work shaped how I
build:
test-driven development, CI/CD pipelines, AWS, and software meant to stay up.
